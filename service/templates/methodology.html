Methodology

Verdicts

Every question is answered with one of three words. YES means positive evidence was observed on chain. no means every evidence source the question depends on was read completely and nothing was found. unknown (incomplete) means a source was missing, truncated, timed out or out of budget. A missing fact never becomes a negative. There are no scores or probabilities.

Separate questions

"Did tracked wallets sell in the window", "is there a direct transaction link", "shared upstream funder", "coordinated trading", "developer connection" and "coordinated launch" are reported separately. A shared service (pool, router, bridge, launchpad) is never counted as a shared owner. Proceeds leaving a wallet are not funding. Transfers to one-way sinks (burn, lockers, stakers) are not sales. Routing hops are counted once. Reverted swaps have no effect. A dead address is not a burn unless the token's burn mechanics say so.

Coverage

Each result states the block it was checked through and lists gaps. Monitoring keeps two cursors: provisional (latest block) and evidence (finalized block). Alerts are sent only for finalized blocks; if a reorg replaces provisional evidence, the replaced items are removed and the canonical ones recorded.

Amounts and time

Raw integer amounts and decimals are preserved in the JSON export. Times are stored in UTC and displayed in America/Los_Angeles with the correct PDT/PST offset.

Wallet drain screen (beta)

Predicates are named after the event that was observed: a transfer event from the victim, an approval event with the victim as owner, an approval-for-all event, a current EIP-7702 delegation. Causes (key compromise, allowance use, delegate action) are always "unknown" because events alone cannot establish them. Native-coin internal transfers, historical delegations, ERC-1155 and Permit2 flows are not indexed and are listed as structural gaps.

Boundaries

We do not publish case pages, name people, file reports, contact exchanges, or request freezes. Those actions need human review and your written authorization and are not part of this product. We never ask for keys, seed phrases or wallet access, and we never ask you to send a transaction from the affected wallet.

Reproducibility

The JSON export carries a manifest (chain id, RPC endpoint, collector and rule versions, covered block ranges, boundary block hashes) and a SHA-256 over the canonical package with the hash field blank, so anyone can recompute it.
