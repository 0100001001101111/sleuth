03 /My wallet was drainedbeta

Paste your wallet address and roughly when it happened. The event screen lists every transfer out and approval in the window. The investigation then fetches each outflow transaction and names how the funds left: an EIP-7702 delegation someone else submitted, an allowance or permit, or the key itself. It profiles the recipients, counts the other wallets the same submitter hit, and checks Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and Robinhood Chain for the same address. It also says whether the account is still delegated, which revoking approvals does not fix. We never need your keys.
