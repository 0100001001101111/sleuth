Disputes and fixes

Corrections.

If a screen or case shows a fact you believe is wrong, or an address you control is described in a way you dispute, we want to know.

What happens

Corrections are reviewed by a person; the on-chain record is re-read and the result, including any reversal, is recorded in the case audit trail and the evidence package versions.

Because case workspaces are private and nothing is published, a correction only changes what the case owner sees. We do not operate a public blacklist and do not accept requests to remove findings for payment.

01 /How to reach us

Use the email on your receipt, or the operator contact in the service configuration. Include the case id and the item key from the evidence table.
